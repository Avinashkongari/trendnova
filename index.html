🛒 Black Friday Deal Guide

Retailers like Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart and others start discounts weeks early, so you don't need to wait for a single day.

Strategy

5 Rules to Avoid Fake Discounts

  1. Check price history with a tracker before you buy.
  2. Compare at least 3 retailers.
  3. Ignore "was" prices; look at the final price.
  4. Read recent reviews, not just star ratings.
  5. Check the return policy and the return window.

How to Choose an AI Assistant

Is Deleting an AI Chat Really Deleting It?

Researchers have shown that traces of local AI app data can sometimes be recovered. Use the app's delete and history-off settings, and avoid pasting passwords or private documents.

📱 Gadgets & Smart Home

Foldable phones, new wearables and Matter-compatible smart home devices are all in the news.

Buying guide

Should You Buy a Foldable Phone?

  • Check the crease and hinge durability reviews.
  • Compare water-resistance ratings.
  • Look at the software-update promise.
  • Consider last year's model on sale.
Smart home

Why "Matter" Support Matters

Matter-compatible devices work across major smart-home platforms, so you're less locked into one brand. Look for the Matter logo when buying plugs, lights and sensors.

🎮 Gaming Corner

Tips

Get Better FPS Without Buying New Hardware

  1. Update your GPU drivers.
  2. Lower shadows and ray-tracing first.
  3. Close background apps and overlays.
  4. Use an SSD for game installs.
Deals

Where Gaming Deals Show Up

🔐 Online Safety

Account-takeover scams are rising. These steps block most attacks.

Must-do

Lock Down Your Accounts in 10 Minutes

  1. Turn on two-factor authentication (app-based if possible).
  2. Use a password manager with unique passwords.
  3. Set up passkeys where offered.
  4. Review logged-in devices and remove unknown ones.
Scam alert

Spotting Fake Holiday Deals

  • Too-good-to-be-true prices on unknown stores.
  • Urgent countdown timers with no real brand.
  • Payment requests by gift card or transfer.
  • Misspelled domains and missing contact info.
VPN

Do You Need a VPN on Public Wi-Fi?

A reputable VPN encrypts your traffic on shared networks. Choose a provider with a clear no-logs policy and independent audits, and avoid free VPNs that monetize your data.

💰 Smart Money for the Holidays

Budget

A Simple Holiday Budget That Works

  1. Total up gifts, travel, food and events.
  2. Set a spending cap per category.
  3. Use a separate account or card for holiday spending.
  4. Track every purchase for 30 days.
Cards & credit

Use Credit Cards Wisely

Rewards are only worth it if you pay the full balance. Carrying a balance costs far more than any cashback you earn.
